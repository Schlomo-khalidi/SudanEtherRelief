Zamzam Camp, North Darfur — 24 Sept 2026 · 14:20 UTC · updated 26 Sept

Famine confirmed in Zamzam displacement camp as access remains blocked

Approved for publication by A. Osman (editor) · 24 Sept, 12:02 UTC · decision log ↓

What happened

The Integrated Food Security Phase Classification formally confirmed famine in Zamzam camp — the first famine determination in Sudan since 2017.S1 · IPCS3 · Sudan Tribune

Aid convoys have been unable to reach the camp since April; the most recent attempt was turned back on 21 September.S1 · IPC

Field teams report more than 300 children admitted for severe malnutrition this week.S2 · Radio Dabanga

Why it matters now

Zamzam hosts roughly half a million displaced people. When a story drops out of the news cycle, the corridor funding that keeps supply lines open follows within weeks. Coverage is not sympathy — it is logistics.

What a donation supports — Ethar-approved

Specifically, this week

$40 — one emergency food parcel for a family of six for two weeks. $240 — a corridor carton run: 6 parcels plus water treatment tablets. Ethar Relief teams are staging from the nearest reachable corridor and rotating stock daily.
